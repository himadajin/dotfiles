## Commits and PRs

- Use Conventional Commits for commit messages and PR titles.
- Write the subject in English, in the imperative mood.
- If the repository's history follows a different convention, follow that instead.

## Prose

- Use semantic line breaks in Markdown and plain-text files you write,
  and in commit bodies and PR descriptions:
  one sentence per line, optionally breaking at clause boundaries.
  Never hard-wrap at a column width.
- In existing files, match the file's current line-break style.
